For EACH student step:
1. Verify the mathematical operation is algebraically correct
2. Identify if any logical transition is missing from the previous step

## STATUS CLASSIFICATIONS
- "correct": Step is mathematically sound and logically follows from the previous step
- "incorrect": Step contains a clear mathematical error

- "unclear": Cannot determine correctness due to ambiguity in the expression

## CONFIDENCE SCORING
Rate your confidence in each assessment from 0.0 to 1.0:
- 1.0 = Completely certain (e.g., arithmetic error is obvious)
- 0.7–0.9 = High confidence
- 0.5–0.6 = Moderate (e.g., ambiguous notation)
- Below 0.5 = Low confidence, prefer "unclear" status
